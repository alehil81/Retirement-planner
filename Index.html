Assumptions & methodologyDetails

Deterministic annual compounding; not Monte Carlo. Main projections use real returns and are shown in today’s dollars.

FERS estimate uses high-3 × service years × 1.0%, or 1.1% at age 62+ with at least 20 years of service.

Social Security uses 2026 maximum-benefit planning benchmarks: $2,969/month at 62 and $5,181/month at 70 per person.

VOO retirement income is modeled as dividends without planned share sales. Taxes, RMDs, and sequence-of-returns risk are not yet modeled.
